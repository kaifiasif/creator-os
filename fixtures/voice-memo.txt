So I was on a walk and thinking about why the review step takes me so long. Um, drafting a post takes me maybe two minutes. Reviewing what the model gave me takes like forty minutes, because I am checking if I actually said the thing.

The real problem is that fluent sentences are the dangerous ones. When a sentence is clunky I fix it right away, but when it reads well I trust it, and that is exactly when it says something I never said.

I think the fix is to check the boring stuff before I even look. Is the claim in my transcript, have I posted this before. A machine can do that in seconds.

And honestly I already wrote a post in March about shipping small. I do not want to say that again with different words.

Last week I rejected 6 of 9 drafts and every single rejection was about voice, not facts.
